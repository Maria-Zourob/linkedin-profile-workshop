Platform Mechanics

The LinkedIn Algorithm Basics

LinkedIn rewards people who create real value and build connections.

LinkedIn Loves:

Consistency

Post regularly and stay active.

Meaningful Comments

Start conversations, not just likes.

Original Content

Share your own experiences and insights.

Engagement

Reply and interact with your network.

LinkedIn Doesn't Like:

Copying content without adding your perspective

Being inactive for long periods

Posting without a clear value or message

Remember: LinkedIn is not about posting more...
it's about creating better conversations.
Content Creation Guide

How to Write Posts on LinkedIn

Follow these simple, effective steps to craft authentic tech posts that engage your audience.

🧠

Start with your own thoughts

Write everything that's on your mind without worrying about grammar or structure.

🗣️

Make it sound like you

Read it again and remove anything that doesn't sound natural or feels too "AI-generated."

🎯

Find the main message

Ask yourself: "What do I want people to learn from this post?"

✨

Add your personal touch

Include:

  • A real experience or challenge faced
  • What you learned & your opinion

This is what makes the post authentic.

🤖

Use AI as an editor

Ask ChatGPT, Gemini, or another AI tool to:

  • Improve clarity & fix grammar
  • Organize ideas & suggest better wording
✅

Review before publishing

Check:

✓ Is hook interesting? ✓ Is it easy to read? ✓ Does it provide value? ✓ Would you read it?
